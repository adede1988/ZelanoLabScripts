**Overall:** Real craft (continuous-then-slice TFR, per-frequency normalization respecting 1/f, session aggregation, FOOOF aperiodic control, and you *built* the pre-inhale baseline I'd demand). But the "goodness" ranking sits on three load-bearing assumptions I don't believe yet: that the ridge tracks a real oscillation, that whole-window z is a fair reference, and that a separation driven by two non-responders means anything. Show me the null distributions.

**Major concerns (ranked):**
1. **Ridge has no guaranteed peak — and usually doesn't** (39% detectable). A forward-backward tracker always returns a ridge in 1/f. "burst," "nBursts," "dutyCycle," "timeAboveMs" are not established as oscillatory until gated by peak-existence/SNR/surrogate.
2. **Lowest-CV winners are tracker artifacts.** `w3_rfreqRaw__mean` = 42.2 Hz, CV 0.045 — the ridge is *bounded* to 25–58 Hz, so a tracker in noise sits near center with tiny variance. goodness ÷(1+CV) *rewards* uninformative bounded estimators.
3. **Separation powered by n≈1–2.** Non-responders = 2 participants (JA, PC); O15 S2/3 non-responder = 1 session/64 breaths. `sep_resp_nonresp` up to 2.10–2.22 drives the ranking while `sep_control_dupi` is 0.94 for #1. The saturated ±0.3 patches in columns 4–5 are sampling noise.
4. **Whole-window z folds the response into its own reference** — mechanically creating the pre-inhale "suppression" in every control panel; magnitude AND sign depend on window length/timing. The claimed `*_base` concordance check is never reported (only `timeAboveMs_base` reaches the top table).
5. **PAC on a non-sinusoidal breath, no surrogate.** `coup_MI` control CV 0.82–1.83 (wildly unstable); Tort MI upward-biased at low breath counts; Rayleigh p is not a coupling test without a time-shifted/breath-shuffled surrogate + harmonic control. `inhExhRatio` low CV but is exactly what airflow/movement produce directly.
6. **Trial-count dependence not removed by session-aggregation** — breaths/session span 76–991; `__var`, `nBursts`, MI are n-dependent *within* each session's statistic.
7. **Superlet resolution undocumented** — report effective FWHM in ms & Hz at 25/40/58 Hz. The descending streak is consistent with onset-transient smearing (temporal support grows as frequency drops).
8. **Edges largely handled** — continuous-then-slice avoids per-epoch edge artifacts (credit); confirm boundary trimming at recording start/end.
