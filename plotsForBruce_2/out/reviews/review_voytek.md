**Overall:** The team saw me coming and did much right (superlet 4e-14, session aggregation, both z-windows, airflow covariates, open code) and — respect — **reported the inconvenient number in bold**: the FOOOF-selected "best gamma" channel had a detectable periodic peak in only **59/150 (39%)** of recordings (O15 35.9%, breathing 44.4%, cue 39.0%, thresh 38.2%). But leading with it doesn't neutralize it: the ridge, the "chirp," and every top measure are built on channels where 6/10 times there is no oscillation to track. Methodologically careful, admirably honest, and currently measuring the aperiodic background while calling it gamma. Major revision.

**Major concerns (ranked):**
(a) **~61% of ridges track the argmax of 1/f, not an oscillation.** The ranking is dominated by ridge-frequency metrics. The tell: `w3_rfreqRaw__mean` (42.2 Hz, sep 2.07) and `w3_rfreqGated__mean` (41.9 Hz, sep 2.10) are nearly identical — gating on peak presence barely moves the ridge → peak and no-peak breaths give the SAME ridge → it's tracking a background centroid, not a gated rhythm.
(b) **Are group differences aperiodic? Not shown.** §10 claims the goodness table includes apExp/gammaBumpDb — it does not. The one analysis that could adjudicate (exponent/offset per group; does separation survive 1/f control) is computed but not shown. iEEG 25–58 Hz "gamma" is largely broadband asynchronous spiking (Manning 2009) = an aperiodic OFFSET shift; a power difference is an E/I/exponent story (Gao 2017) until proven otherwise.
(c) **Within-frequency z normalizes the 1/f shape away** — suppressing the broadband/offset (aperiodic) hypothesis and preserving the transient one, then concluding transient. Show the raw aperiodic params.
(d) **Selection rule + FOOOF config.** "Max flattened power" is maximized by fit misspecification/spikes, not oscillations. Concrete: control **AZ** cueTask macBP5 = 18.37 dB flattened, gammaDetected=0, while **3 non-selected channels had detectable peaks** — the rule picked the no-peak channel over three with peaks. 60 Hz mains sits inside F=22:62 (2 Hz above the 58 ceiling); notch removed → leakage at 55–58 a first-order suspect. No knee over a 40-Hz-wide range (Gerster 2022); no R² for per-breath FOOOF-lite on 2–5 s breaths; `spikeFrac` up to 0.238 (JL_2 thresh) — spikes manufacture in-band "peaks."
(e) **The "chirp" is a transient, not a sweep.** Control/responder feature is broad (~38–55 Hz) at t≈0 decaying diagonally to ~28 Hz over ~1.5 s. A sweeping oscillation is narrowband at each instant; this is broad-then-descending = the impulse response of a frequency-dependent-resolution bank (longer wavelets at low freq → energy later & lower) to a sharp inhale-locked deflection.

**Actionable:** (1) gate every ridge/burst on a per-breath specparam peak, re-rank; (2) report apExp/offset per group and test whether control↔Dupi and resp↔non-resp survive regressing out apExp+offset (or use periodic power above fit); (3) refit FOOOF with a knee, mask 58–62 Hz, report R², drop bad fits, recompute detectability; (4) **simulate the null** — pass a broadband inhale delta through the identical superlet+ridge and show it reproduces the ~42 Hz descending ridge with no oscillation; (5) fix selection rule (require in-band peak / rank by periodic power; exclude high spikeFrac); (6) drop the units artifact (rank on standardized effects, stop taking max over 3 tests, flag n<3 cells).

The z-power/AUC measures are offset-sensitive and point at Manning 2009, not a rhythm. Recovery weak (rho 0.13–0.27); responder axis is two people. Settle it with: (1) peak-gated re-ranking, (2) apExp/offset per group with separation tested after 1/f control, (3) the broadband-transient simulation.
